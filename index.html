Our story

Good coffee, honest food, a place that feels like yours.

Built around the cup.

We started with a simple idea: Nairobi deserves a café that takes coffee seriously without taking itself too seriously. Great espresso, warm milk poured with care, and a menu of breakfast plates, pastries and light meals that go with it.

Our space is designed for the way people actually use cafés: a quiet corner to work, a long table for friends, a quick takeaway when the day is moving fast.
